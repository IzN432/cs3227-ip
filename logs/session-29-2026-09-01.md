## Actions

  `PauseTransition`.
- Added CSS: `#userLabel { -fx-text-fill: #ededed; }`, `#balanceLabel { -fx-text-fill: #d9b77a; }`.
- Added `MarketplaceTest` cases for buy (6 tests) and bid (8 tests).
